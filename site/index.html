Get on the T

Squash club nights, organised. Players of similar ability on court, fair court time for everyone, and nobody left on the bench too long.

Coming to Android and iPhone

Who goes on which court next?

The one question at the desk on a busy club night, answered for you. You always have the last word.

People come and go

Check players in as they arrive at either start time and tap them out when they leave. The queue adjusts itself.

Courts come and go

Lose a court mid-game and the players who were cut short go back on first.

Fair, and you can see it

Court time is measured against the time each person was actually there. Anyone waiting too long turns amber, then red.

Courts change one at a time

Games are staggered so one court frees at a time, with the next game already called to the door.

Next: tournaments

Team round robins, then knockouts with ability divisions, built on the same players, courts and fairness.
