Support

Found a problem, or have an idea for a feature? Open an issue on GitHub. If you don't use GitHub, email robvanstone+getonthet@gmail.com.

Please never include anyone's name or other personal details. Issues on GitHub are public.

Common questions

Where is my club's data?

On your phone, and nowhere else. Nothing is sent to us. See the privacy policy.

How does it decide who plays next?

Everyone earns a fair share of court time while they are there. The app puts first the people who have waited longest on average and who are owed the most court time, and pairs them with someone of similar ability they haven't already played tonight. You can change any suggestion.

Does the timer keep going if I lock the phone?

Yes. Times are worked out from when each game started, so a locked phone or a closed app loses nothing.

How long are the games?

About 10 minutes on a busy night, stretching towards 20 as people leave. You can change any game's length.
