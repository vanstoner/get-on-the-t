# Privacy policy: Get on the T

*Last updated 9 October 2026. Applies to the app **Get on the T: Squash Club
Organiser** on Android and iPhone.*

Get on the T helps the person running a squash club night put players of
similar ability on court and keep court time fair. It is designed so that the
developer never receives any information about you or your club's members.

## What the app holds

Only what the organiser types in or records during an evening:

- **Players' display names**, as the organiser chooses to write them, and an
  **ability level** from 1 to 10. The app has no place for contact details,
  dates of birth or photos.
- **Courts**, by their labels (for example "Court 3").
- **What happened on each evening:** who was expected and who arrived, breaks
  and departures, which courts were open, and which games were called and
  played, with their times. From this the app works out court time, waits and
  fairness. There are no scores.

## Where it is kept

**On the organiser's phone, and nowhere else.** The app has no account, no
server and no analytics, and it sends nothing over the internet. On Android
the app opts out of the phone's automatic cloud backup, so members' names do
not leave the phone that way either.

If a later version lets the organiser export a file or share a schedule with
other phones, it will happen only when the organiser chooses to, this policy
will be updated first, and the app will say so before anything leaves the
phone.

## Who is responsible for the data

The organiser who uses the app decides what to record about their members and
is responsible for it, as they would be for a paper list. The developer never
receives it and cannot see, change or delete it.

## Deleting it

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it holds.

## Children

The app is a tool for an adult organiser. It collects nothing from the people
whose names are entered, of any age.
